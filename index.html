T-Rex Run 3D

Experience the thrill of running as a T-Rex in this 3D pixel-style endless runner game. Jump over obstacles, collect coins, and set new high scores!

Gameplay

Control your T-Rex dinosaur as it runs through a prehistoric landscape. Your goal is to run as far as possible while avoiding obstacles and collecting coins.

How to Play

  • Jump: Press SPACEBAR or UP ARROW to jump over obstacles
  • Duck: Press DOWN ARROW to slide under low obstacles
  • Double Jump: Press jump twice in mid-air for extra height
  • Collect Coins: Gather coins to unlock new dinosaur skins
  • Avoid Obstacles: Cacti, rocks, and other dinosaurs will end your run
  • Power-ups: Collect special items for temporary advantages

Features

  • 3D pixel graphics with retro charm
  • Multiple dinosaur characters to unlock
  • Increasing difficulty as you progress
  • Special power-ups and bonuses
  • Global leaderboard to compete with friends
  • Day/Night cycle for varied gameplay
  • Special events and seasonal content

Tips

  • Time your jumps carefully - obstacles come in different heights
  • Collect coins to unlock special abilities
  • Watch for power-ups that give temporary invincibility
  • Practice makes perfect - learn the patterns!
  • Try to memorize obstacle sequences for better reaction times
  • Use headphones for immersive audio cues

Scoring

Earn points for every meter you run. Bonus points are awarded for collecting coins and power-ups. The longer you survive, the higher your score multiplier becomes!

Unlockables

As you play, you'll earn coins that can be used to unlock new dinosaur skins, each with unique visual styles. Some rare skins provide special visual effects!
